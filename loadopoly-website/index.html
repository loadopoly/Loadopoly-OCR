The problem

80–90% of historical data is
invisible to AI

Institutions have spent decades digitizing documents, but the data remains flat, inconsistent, and unqueryable. AI models have a hard cutoff at what's been indexed — everything older is dark.

Today's reality

  • Millions of digitized items stored as flat JSON with no relationships
  • 35+ inconsistent schemas across archives — impossible to join
  • No entity extraction, no GPS, no semantic search
  • Researchers spend 40+ hours per collection on manual curation
  • LLMs trained on post-2010 web data — "data wall" arriving 2026–27

GeoGraph OCR solves this

  • Automatic entity extraction from any photo or scan (people, places, dates, orgs)
  • Knowledge graph auto-built with semantic cross-document links
  • GPS & GIS metadata enrichment on every document
  • Reduce curation from 40 hours to under 5 minutes
  • Creates licensable, structured AI training datasets
Capabilities

Everything you need to unlock dark data

Built on Google Gemini 2.5 Flash with a privacy-first, offline-capable architecture.

AI-Powered OCR

Capture text from historical documents, artifacts, signs, and scenery. Multi-language detection included. Runs on Gemini, OpenAI, or local models.

Knowledge Graph

Automatically links entities across documents. Interactive force-directed D3.js visualization. Export to JSON, CSV, or graph formats.

GIS Metadata

Enrich every scan with GPS coordinates, zone classification, and historical location correlation. Normalized to WGS84.

3D Metaverse Explore

Navigate your entire document corpus in an immersive 3D spatial environment. Semantic clustering groups related records visually.

Smart Deduplication

Semantic NLP detects and merges duplicate entities across thousands of documents — no manual cleanup required.

AI Training Marketplace

License your structured datasets to AI companies. Users earn passive income as fractional owners when their data is licensed.
