Features

Everything you need to digitize history

A complete platform for researchers, archivists, and historians to capture, process, and share historical records.

AI-Powered OCR

Extract text from historical documents, artifacts, and scenery. Automatic entity recognition for people, places, dates, and organizations.

Knowledge Graph Generation

Automatically build semantic relationships between documents. Interactive force-directed graph visualization powered by D3.js.

GIS Metadata Enrichment

Capture geographic context with device GPS. Zone classification, historical location correlation, and WGS84 coordinate normalization.

3D Metaverse Navigation

Explore your document corpus in immersive 3D with spatial clustering. Optional AR camera overlay for real-world scanning.

Batch Processing

Process hundreds of documents at once with a server-side queue. Real-time progress tracking with pause, resume, and cancel controls.

Privacy-First Architecture

All data stored locally by default with IndexedDB. Optional encrypted cloud sync. End-to-end encryption for sensitive records.

How It Works

From photo to structured data in minutes

No special equipment needed — just your phone camera and Loadopoly.

1

Capture

Take a photo of any historical document, artifact, or sign using the in-app camera or upload existing images.

2

Process

Google Gemini 2.5 Flash extracts text and identifies entities — people, places, dates, and organizations — automatically.

3

Connect

The platform links related documents into a knowledge graph and enriches each record with GPS coordinates and metadata.

4

Share or License

Export your structured dataset, sync to the cloud, or license it to AI research organizations through our marketplace.
